# Privacy Statement

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

Lucide is an open-source project. We built lucide.dev to collect as little about you as possible: no accounts, no tracking cookies, and no selling of data. This page explains what does happen when you visit, and why.

This statement covers **lucide.dev** only. Our merch shop (merch.lucide.dev), GitHub, Discord, Open Collective and other sites we link to have their own privacy policies.

## Who is responsible

lucide.dev is run by the Lucide Icons, based in the Netherlands. For questions about this statement or your data, contact us at [info@lucide.dev](mailto:info@lucide.dev).

## What we collect and why

### Hosting

The site runs on [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy). Like any web server, Vercel processes your IP address, browser user agent and the pages you request so it can deliver the site and protect it against abuse. These logs are stored for 1 day and are not used to profile you.

### Analytics

We use privacy-friendly analytics to see which pages and icons are popular, so we know what to improve. We run two tools on our own subdomains, and the data they collect is stored in the EU:

- **Plausible** (analytics.lucide.dev) counts page views.
- **OpenPanel** (insights.lucide.dev) counts page views and how the site is used: which icons are copied or opened in Lucide Studio, when an icon search starts, changes to the icon customiser and sorting, clicks on the home page buttons and packages, and how far down a page you scroll.

Neither tool sets cookies or stores anything in your browser. Along with these events they record general information such as the referring site, browser type, device type and country. Visits are counted with a temporary, anonymised identifier that cannot be used to recognise you on other sites or on later days. We don't track clicks on outgoing links, and we never combine this data with other sources.

### Search

Site search is provided by [Algolia DocSearch](https://www.algolia.com/policies/privacy/). When you use the search box, what you type and your IP address are sent to Algolia to return results. Your recent searches are kept in your own browser (see below).

### Advertising

On some pages, a single ad is shown by [Carbon Ads](https://www.carbonads.net/privacy) (BuySellAds). The ads help fund the project's development. Ads are chosen by the content of the page, not by your interests or browsing history. To show an ad, count impressions and clicks, and prevent fraud, Carbon receives your IP address (used to estimate your country) and basic browser information. Carbon's privacy policy describes how it handles this data.

### Third-party images

Contributor and team avatars are loaded directly from GitHub, so GitHub receives your IP address when those images load. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The version and download badges on the packages page are loaded from [Shields.io](https://shields.io), so Shields.io receives your IP address when those images load.

## Cookies and browser storage

lucide.dev does not use tracking or advertising cookies of its own.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `__vdpl` | Cookie (Vercel) | Keeps you on the same version of the site while it's being updated, so pages don't break mid-visit | Session |
| `icon-size`, `icon-stroke-width`, `icon-color`, `icon-non-scaling-stroke` | Local storage | Remembers your icon customiser settings | Until you clear them |
| `vitepress-theme-appearance` | Local storage | Remembers light or dark mode | Until you clear them |
| `lucide-docs-fallback-framework` and code tab choices | Local storage | Remembers which framework you're reading the docs for | Until you clear them |
| DocSearch recent searches | Local storage | Shows your recent searches in the search box | Until you clear them |

These items are needed for features you use, or they store choices you made yourself. They never leave your browser, and you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

## Legal basis

We process this data on the basis of our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in running a fast, secure and sustainable website: hosting and protecting the site, understanding how it is used, and funding the project through non-tracking ads. We keep data minimal and avoid identifying individual visitors.

## International transfers

Vercel, Algolia, Carbon Ads, GitHub and Shields.io may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where this happens, these providers rely on safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Because we don't link analytics data to individuals, we usually can't find data that belongs to you specifically, but we're happy to help. Contact us at [info@lucide.dev](mailto:info@lucide.dev).

You also have the right to file a complaint with a data protection authority. In the Netherlands, that is the [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

## Changes

If we add new services or change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. The full history of this page is available in our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/commits/main/docs/privacy.md).
